Domiqo Account Deletion

Domiqo is provided by Jerick Technology Ltd. This page explains how Domiqo users can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Contact Jerick Technology Ltd using the contact details provided on the Domiqo website.
  2. Use the email address associated with your Domiqo account.
  3. State that you would like your Domiqo account and associated data to be deleted.
  4. We may ask you to verify your identity before processing the request.

Data that will be deleted

When an account deletion request is completed, personal data associated with the Domiqo account will be deleted where applicable, including account information and user-created household data stored by Domiqo.

This may include household information, documents, maintenance information, inventory information, expense information, pet information, photos and other information associated with the account.

Data that may be retained

Certain information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution or regulatory purposes. Any retained information will only be kept for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Contact

For account or data deletion requests, please contact Jerick Technology Ltd through the contact information provided on the Domiqo website.

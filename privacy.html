Domiqo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Domiqo is provided by Jerick Technology Ltd. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored and protected when you use the Domiqo mobile application and related services.

Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Domiqo may process information such as your account information, email address, household information, home-related information, documents, maintenance information, inventory information, family expense information, pet information, photos and other information that you choose to provide.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide and operate Domiqo features, authenticate users, save and synchronise user data, enable household sharing, provide reminders and notifications, and maintain and improve the service.

Photos, Camera and Files

Domiqo may request access to your camera, photos or files when you choose features such as document scanning, uploading documents, adding pet photos, recording home information or scanning QR codes. These permissions are used only to provide the features you request.

Data Storage and Service Providers

Domiqo may use third-party service providers, including cloud infrastructure and authentication services, to securely operate features of the application. Information may be processed by these providers as necessary to provide the service.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate Domiqo, or where required by law. Information that you intentionally share through household or family-sharing features may be accessible to the people you choose to share it with.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service and meet applicable legal requirements. Users may request deletion of their personal information by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

Domiqo is intended for household management and is not designed for children to create or independently manage accounts.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, correct or request deletion of your information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Domiqo or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to make a privacy or data deletion request, please contact Jerick Technology Ltd.
